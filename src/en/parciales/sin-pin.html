The Tarjeta Familia has no PIN

If the card terminal asks for a PIN, press the green key and the payment goes ahead. It is confirmed with your signature.

If staff in a shop, or even on the customer helpline, tell you that you need a PIN, you can show them the official terms of the Junta de Castilla y León:

«Se trata de una tarjeta de banda magnética, que no requiere PIN para realizar las compras (en caso de que se pida el PIN en el terminal de pago hay que pulsar la tecla verde para continuar la compra). Se autentica a través de firma. No es posible el uso mediante contacless.»

In English: “It is a magnetic stripe card that does not require a PIN for purchases (if the payment terminal asks for a PIN, press the green key to continue). It is authenticated by signature. Contactless use is not possible.”

Source: Terms and instructions for use of the Tarjeta Familia (Spanish), section 2 (16 October 2026).
