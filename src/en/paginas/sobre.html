Why this site exists

It is a citizen initiative, made by a family that uses the card. It exists because the Junta de Castilla y León's official information is hard to find, understand and use, especially on a mobile phone.

This is not an official website. It is not linked to the Junta de Castilla y León, CaixaBank or MoneyToPay, and it does not manage the grant. All content comes from the Junta's public documents. If in doubt, the official information prevails.

What is wrong with the official information

  • It is spread across 7 PDF documents. The Junta's page about the Tarjeta Familia has hardly any text: to find out how to activate it, pay, check the balance or where to use it, you have to download and read PDFs.
  • The list of shops is a 41-page PDF, ordered by province and town, with no search and no distances. To find a pharmacy near home you have to go through it by hand.
  • The most useful fact is hidden. The card also works in shops that aren't on the list, if their terminal is in an authorised business type. That only appears in the section for shops.
  • The PDFs are not accessible: they are not prepared for screen readers and don't adapt to mobile screens. Public-sector websites must comply with Royal Decree 1112/2018 and standard EN 301 549 (WCAG 2.1 AA).
  • The data contain errors: the total in the list's summary doesn't match its rows (1,644 versus 1,628), some shops are repeated and some towns are misspelt. See the original documents.
  • The information doesn't reach every channel. When we called CaixaBank's helpline, the person who answered didn't know the card has no PIN, even though the official terms say so explicitly.
  • Confusing names. The guides from MoneyToPay, the card issuer, call it “Tarjeta Regalo” (gift card) and “Tarjeta Empresa” (company card).
  • Everything is in Spanish only, even though many families in Castilla y León are more comfortable in other languages.

What this site does

  • Brings everything together in web pages, organised by what you need to do: families and shops.
  • Offers a shop finder by town, municipality, postcode or location, which always shows the nearest shops.
  • Is designed for mobile phones and to meet the WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility guidelines.
  • Is available in Spanish, English and Arabic. The translations are this site's own: the official documents exist only in Spanish and prevail in case of doubt.
  • Uses no cookies and does not store your location.

Where the data come from

  • Partner shops: the Junta's official list dated {{fecha_listado}} ({{total_establecimientos}} shops, without duplicates).
  • Terms of use: official document dated 16 October 2026. MoneyToPay guides to activating the card and checking the balance.
  • Authorised business types: official document dated 25 June 2024.
  • Municipalities and postcodes: National Geographic Institute (IGN) and National Centre for Geographic Information (CNIG). Villages and hamlets: Electoral Census street directory (INE). Coordinates: CartoCiudad (IGN) and GeoNames.

When the Junta publishes a new list, it is processed again and the site is updated. Distances are approximate: the official list doesn't include each shop's location, so shops are placed by their address where possible and, otherwise, at the centre of their town.

Found a mistake?

If a shop no longer accepts the card, one is missing or something is wrong, let us know on GitHub. The site's code is open source.
