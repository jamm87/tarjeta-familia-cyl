How to pay

The Tarjeta Familia doesn't work quite like a bank card. Here is what to know before you reach the till.

At the till

  1. Make sure you have enough balance

    If the purchase is more than your balance, the whole payment is declined. How to check your balance.

  2. Ask for the magnetic stripe to be swiped

    The card doesn't work contactless: tapping it on the terminal won't work.

  3. If the terminal asks for a PIN, press the green key

    {{sin_pin}}

  4. Sign the receipt

    The purchase is confirmed with your signature.

  5. Keep the receipt

    For 6 months, with the products itemised. Why.

In short

  • ✕No: cash withdrawalsNeither at cash machines nor as a cash refund when you return something.
  • ✕No: contactlessNeither tapping the card nor paying with your phone.
  • !No PIN: green keyIf the terminal asks for a PIN, press the green key.
