Check your balance

It's worth checking before a large purchase: if the amount is more than your balance, the payment is declined.

On your phone, with the MoneyToPay app

The easiest way to check your balance before you pay.

  1. Search for “MoneyToPay” in your phone's app store: Google Play on Android or the App Store on iPhone. Make sure it is the official MoneyToPay app before entering your details. The app is in Spanish.
  2. Log in with your MoneyToPay username and password, the same ones you created when you activated the card.
  3. You will see the card with its available balance and transactions.

On the website, with your MoneyToPay account

  1. Log in on www.moneytopay.com with your username and password.
  2. Open «Mis tarjetas» (my cards): you will see the available balance.
  3. To see transactions, click the three dots under the card number.

On the website, without an account

  1. On www.moneytopay.com, click «Consulta tu saldo» (check your balance).
  2. Enter the card details, tick «No soy un robot» (I'm not a robot) and click «Consultar» (check).

At a CaixaBank cash machine

You can check the balance and transactions at any CaixaBank cash machine. You can't withdraw money.
