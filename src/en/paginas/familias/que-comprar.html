What you can buy, and where

The card is meant for the basic costs of looking after your children, in shops in Castilla y León.

What you can buy

  • ✓Baby and children's foodFormula milk, baby food, cereals…
  • ✓Nappies and hygiene products
  • ✓Children's clothing and accessories
  • ✓Pharmacy and orthopaedic products
  • ✓Other products for childcare

Use it only for these. If it is spent on anything else, the Junta may ask you to repay the grant.

Where you can use it

In physical shops in Castilla y León, of two kinds:

  1. Partner shops. They have signed up voluntarily and usually display the sticker «AQUÍ puedes utilizar la Tarjeta Familia» (you can use the Tarjeta Familia HERE). There are currently {{total_establecimientos}}.
    Find partner shops near you
  2. Any other shop in an authorised business type (pharmacies, opticians, supermarkets, food shops, baby shops, children's clothing…), even if it is not a partner. What matters is the business type its card terminal is registered under.
    See the authorised business types

Keep receipts for 6 months

Keep the receipt or invoice for every purchase for 6 months from the date of payment, with the products itemised. The authorities may ask for them at any time to check that the money was spent as intended.

A practical tip: take a photo of each receipt with your phone as you leave the shop. Thermal paper fades over time.
