Authorised business types

The Tarjeta Familia works in any shop whose card terminal is registered under one of these {{total_sectores}} business types, whether or not it is a partner shop.

Don't know which business type your terminal is under? Ask your bank: it can confirm the type used to process your payments.

The codes are those of the card payment networks: ES is the national code and IN the international one (MCC). Descriptions are given in Spanish, as in the official document.

For example: farmacia (pharmacy), juguetes (toys), calzado (shoes) or 5912
