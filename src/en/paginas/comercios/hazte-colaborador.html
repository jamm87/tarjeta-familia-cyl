Become a partner shop

It's free and voluntary. If you authorise publication, your shop will appear in the Junta's official list and, from there, in this shop finder.

You don't need to be a partner to accept the card. It's enough for your terminal to be in an authorised business type. Being a partner is mainly about families being able to find you.

If you have several shops, fill in one form for each.

This website doesn't store or send your data: at the end, an email is prepared for you to send from your own account to the Junta de Castilla y León (conciliacion.gss@jcyl.es), which handles registrations. The email is written in Spanish, as the Junta requires.

    What happens next
    
          
    Your application goes to the Regional Ministry for Families. If you authorise publication, your shop will be added to the list of partner shops and to this shop finder.
    
          
    For any questions, email conciliacion.gss@jcyl.es.
