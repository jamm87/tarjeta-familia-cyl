How to take payment with the Tarjeta Familia

One page for till staff. Print it and keep it next to the card terminal.

Step by step

  1. Swipe the magnetic stripe

    The card doesn't work contactless.

  2. If the terminal asks for a PIN, press the green key

    {{sin_pin}}

  3. Ask the customer to sign

    The purchase is authenticated by signature, not by PIN.

  4. Give an itemised receipt

    The family must keep it for 6 months to account for the spending.

Keep in mind

  • ✕It can't be used in your online shopOnly in the physical shop.
  • ✕No cash refundsNot even if the customer returns a product.
  • !If the amount exceeds the balance, it is declinedThe family can check the balance on moneytopay.com or at a CaixaBank cash machine.
  • !It is for children's productsBaby food, nappies, hygiene, children's clothing and accessories, pharmacy, orthopaedics and other childcare products.

Card declined?

  • Check that the stripe was swiped and that the green key was pressed if a PIN was requested.
  • Your terminal may not be in an authorised business type: ask your bank.
  • The family may not have enough balance, or the card may not be activated.
