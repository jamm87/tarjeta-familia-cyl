Where to use the card

Search the 1,623 partner shops in Castilla y León (list dated 21 October 2026).

Not only in these shops. You can also pay in any physical shop in Castilla y León in an authorised business type (pharmacies, supermarkets, opticians, children's clothing shops…), even if it doesn't appear here.

    Suggestions appear as you type. Use the arrow keys to choose one.
