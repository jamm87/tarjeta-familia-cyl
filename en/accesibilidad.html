Accessibility

This website aims to meet level AA of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, as required of public-sector websites by standard EN 301 549 and Royal Decree 1112/2018.

What we have taken into account

  • All the information is on the page, not in PDFs. The original documents are only linked for reference.
  • Plain language: short sentences, numbered steps and a single name, “Tarjeta Familia”.
  • Three languages: Spanish, English and Arabic. The Arabic version is displayed right to left.
  • It can be used with a keyboard alone and is marked up for screen readers (headings, landmarks, labels and status messages).
  • The shop finder works without the map: the list contains all the information and announces how many results there are.
  • Large text and good contrast (at least 4.5:1), which can be zoomed to 200% without losing content.
  • Designed mobile first, with no horizontal scrolling at 320 pixels wide.
  • Buttons and links with a touch target of at least 44 × 44 pixels.
  • Forms with visible labels, hints and errors explained next to each field and in a summary.
  • It respects the system setting to reduce motion.
  • The practical guides can be printed.
  • No cookies are used and your location is not stored. Your browser only remembers whether you prefer to see the map.

Known limitations

  • The map uses the Leaflet library and OpenStreetMap tiles. It is an extra: everything it shows is also in the list.
  • Distances are approximate: the official list has no coordinates, so each shop is placed by its address where possible and, otherwise, at the centre of its town.
  • The original PDF documents linked here are not accessible and are only in Spanish.
  • Shop names, addresses and business type descriptions are shown in Spanish, as in the official sources.
  • It has not yet had a full manual audit or testing with real screen readers. If you find a barrier, let us know.

Automated checks

Every page is checked with axe-core (WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 rules, levels A and AA). See tests/probar.mjs in the repository.
