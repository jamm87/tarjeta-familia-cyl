Help and contact

Three organisations are involved in the Tarjeta Familia — the Junta, CaixaBank and MoneyToPay — and each handles something different. This table tells you who to contact. Phone lines and emails are answered in Spanish.

Who to contact for each problem
If you need to…Contact
Report a lost, stolen or faulty cardCaixaBank: 900 40 40 90, and email conciliacion.gss@jcyl.es.
Remember: the card has no PIN. If you are told otherwise, see what to do.
