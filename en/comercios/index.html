I run a shop

What you need to know if a family wants to pay with the Tarjeta Familia in your shop, and how to sign up as a partner.

Can I accept the Tarjeta Familia?

Yes, if your card terminal is registered under an authorised business type: pharmacies, opticians, supermarkets and food shops, clothing and shoes, toys, baby products, health, education…

You don't need to be a partner shop to accept it.

Check my business type

If you don't know which business type your terminal is registered under, your bank can tell you.

  • Authorised business typesFind your type of business among the 116 accepted.

What do I gain as a partner shop?

  • ✓Families can find youYou appear in the shop finder and in the Junta's official list.

  • ✓Free and voluntaryAll it takes is one form.
