What the Tarjeta Familia is

It is how the Junta de Castilla y León pays the Birth Grant (Bono Nacimiento) and, where so decided, other family support.

How it works

  • It is a prepaid card: it is loaded with the amount of the grant and you spend that balance.
  • You use it to pay for products and services for looking after your children in shops in Castilla y León.
  • It is not a credit card and it is not linked to your bank account.

How it arrives

First you need to apply for the Birth Grant. Once the regional Social Services department (Gerencia de Servicios Sociales) approves it, the card is sent to your home by registered post, in the name of the first parent on the application. The first thing to do when it arrives is to activate it.

Since 16 October 2026 the full amount is loaded in one go. If your grant was approved earlier and you have only received half, the other half will be paid onto the same card before 31 December 2026.

Who issues it

It is issued by Global Payments MoneyToPay EDE, S.L. (MoneyToPay) and distributed by CaixaBank on behalf of the Junta. That is why, depending on what you need, you will deal with either MoneyToPay or CaixaBank.

About the names

On MoneyToPay's website and guides the card appears as «Tarjeta Regalo» (gift card) or «Tarjeta Empresa» (company card). It is the same Tarjeta Familia; these are the generic names MoneyToPay uses for this type of card.

Your data

MoneyToPay is the controller of the data you provide when activating the card. It uses them to manage the card, meet its legal obligations and prevent fraud (identification and financial data). You can exercise your rights at caixabank.com/ejerciciodederechos and read its privacy policy (both in Spanish).

The Regional Ministry for Families may see the payments you make with the card in order to resolve incidents.
