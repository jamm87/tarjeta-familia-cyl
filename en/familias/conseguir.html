How to get the card

You don't apply for the Tarjeta Familia on its own: it is sent to you when the Junta awards you the Birth Grant (Bono Nacimiento), a payment for each child born or adopted. This page explains the 2026 call for applications.

You have 3 months from the birth or adoption

The 2026 call covers children born or adopted between 1 January and 31 December 2026. Your application must be registered within 3 months of the birth or adoption, even if that period ends in 2027. Late applications are refused.

For children born between 1 January and 2 March 2026, the period ran from 2 March to 2 June 2026. The additional disability payment has its own deadline: 3 months from the decision recognising the disability.

The application, the documents and all official communication are in Spanish only. Below, official terms are given in Spanish where it helps you recognise them.

Who can apply

Families with a child born in 2026, or an adoption finalised in 2026, who meet all of the following:

  • The parents are legally resident in Spain and hold a valid document proving it (DNI or TIE).
  • The parents were registered on the municipal register (padrón) of a town in Castilla y León on the date of birth.
  • The baby is registered on the padrón in Castilla y León.
  • They are up to date with tax and Social Security payments.
  • They have not lost parental authority, and their children are not in the care of a public institution.

Single-parent families. The sole responsible person is the parent who holds sole parental authority, and also a woman recognised as a victim of gender-based violence, even if she does not hold sole parental authority.

Adoptions. Only the adoption of a child in the care of a Spanish public child protection body, and international adoptions. What counts is the date the adoption ruling becomes final, not the date it is entered in the Civil Registry.

The grant is awarded in the name of the person listed as first parent on the application. The card is sent to that person, who will need to activate it.

How much you get

It depends on the family's income and on the baby's position among the children.

First€1,500€1,000€500
Second€2,000€1,500€1,000
Third and subsequent€2,500€2,000€1,500
  • Income is the sum of both parents' general and savings tax bases in their 2024 income tax (IRPF) return, filed in 2025. If a parent was not required to file, the income data held by the Tax Agency are used.
  • The child number counts the children of both parents, including those each has from previous relationships.
  • Disability: the amount is doubled if the baby has a recognised disability of 33% or more. This part can be claimed with the application or later, before the child turns five, under the calls of the following years.
  • Multiple births or simultaneous adoptions: each child receives the highest amount that applies to its position, within the family's income band.

Since 16 October 2026 the amount is loaded onto the card in one go. Previously, half was paid at the start and half six months later; if your grant was paid that way and the second half is still due, it will be added to the same card before 31 December 2026.

The Birth Grant is not taxed: it is exempt income for IRPF purposes.

Grant or tax deduction: you have to choose

The Birth Grant is not compatible with the regional income tax deduction for birth or adoption. If you receive the grant, you cannot claim that deduction in your 2026 tax return, filed in 2027. It is compatible with all other support.

Depending on your income and circumstances, one may be better for you than the other, and the Junta leaves the choice to each family. These are the deduction amounts published by the Junta:

  • The deduction is higher in rural areas. The Junta publishes the list of municipalities that count as urban.
  • For multiple births or adoptions the deduction increases (by 50% for two children and 100% for three), and you can also deduct €901 in each of the following two years.
  • For adoptions there is also a deduction for adoption costs: €784 for national and €3,625 for international adoptions.

For reference: for a first child, the grant ranges from €500 to €1,500 depending on income, and the deduction is €1,010 (€1,420 in rural areas). Check the conditions of the deduction on the Junta's tax website (in Spanish): urban areas, rural areas and summary.

Once awarded, the grant can only be given up if the circumstances that led to it change; for example, if the family moves out of Castilla y León and can no longer use the card.

Documents

The Junta can check your identity (Police), your padrón registration (INE), your income (Tax Agency) and, where relevant, the baby's disability itself, unless you object in the application. If you object, you must give a reason and provide those documents yourself. Even so, you must always attach:

  1. The baby's full birth certificate (certificado literal de nacimiento) from the Civil Registry. The Junta asks you to upload it even if you allow it to check your data.
  2. For the family's other children: the family book (libro de familia) listing all of them or, if there isn't one, their birth certificates, plus their DNI or NIE if they have one.
  3. The baby's certificate of registration on the padrón (certificado de empadronamiento).
  4. For the adoption of a child born before 2026: the adoption ruling and the Civil Registry entry certificate.
  5. If the sole responsible person is a woman who is a victim of gender-based violence: the document proving it under article 7 of Castilla y León Law 13/2010.
  6. If either parent was taxed under a special IRPF scheme, in the Basque Country or in Navarre: a copy of the 2024 tax return or the 2024 income data certificate.

If the application is incomplete, you will be given 10 days to complete it. Otherwise it is closed.

How to apply

  1. Fill in the application in the e-government website's online form

    You must use the Birth Grant application form (in Spanish), even if you then submit it on paper. A paper document without an application recorded in the online form is not valid. All the official information is on the procedure page of the e-government website.

    • There is one application per family. If both parents hold parental authority, both must appear on it and sign it.
    • The form assigns an application number. Note it down: you use it to check the status.
    • It can't be downloaded until all the details are recorded. Don't reuse that number for another family's application: only the last details saved count.

    Before you start, check the technical requirements of the e-government website.

  2. Sign it and submit it

    It depends on how many parents have an electronic signature (digital certificate, electronic DNI or Cl@ve permanente):

    • Both: you both sign in the online form, which sends it to the electronic register. This is the most direct route.
    • Only one: download the application, print it and both sign it by hand. The parent with an electronic signature scans it and registers it attached to the «modelo de subsanación y/o aportación de datos» (form for providing additional information), or on another public body's electronic register. You can also take it to an in-person register office.
    • Neither: download the application, print it, sign it by hand and submit it at an in-person register office, preferably the regional Social Services office (Gerencia Territorial de Servicios Sociales) in your province. Any Junta register office or other official register, such as post offices, also accepts it.

    A paper application with crossings-out or corrections, or one that doesn't match what was recorded online, is not valid.

  3. Attach the documents

    In the online form, or on paper with the application if you submit it in person. If something is missing, you can provide it later using the additional information form.

Submitting the application means accepting the grant if it is awarded, although you can appeal against the amount.

After you apply

  • Applications are decided in order of arrival, from the moment the file is complete. That is why it pays to submit all the documents from the start.
  • The Junta publishes regular decisions (almost monthly in 2026) on the procedure page and on the Birth Grant page, with lists of grants awarded, grants refused and the reasons. The decision of 1 October 2026 covered applications registered before 20 September.
  • The maximum period is 6 months from the date your application is registered. If no decision is published by then, the application is deemed refused.
  • The decision is notified by post or, if you gave an email address, with a notice to read it on the NOTI notifications system.
  • If it is awarded, the card arrives by post at the address you gave, about eight weeks after the decision. The next step is to activate it.
  • Meanwhile, report any change in your family situation that could affect the grant.

Why applications are refused

These are the reasons for refusal the Junta publishes with its decisions. Most can be avoided by checking the application before submitting it:

  1. The relationship, or the fact that the baby was born or adopted in 2026, is not proven. Attach the birth certificate and the family book.
  2. A parent does not have a valid DNI or NIE. Renew it first if it has expired.
  3. Neither parent was on the padrón in Castilla y León on the date of birth.
  4. The baby is not on the padrón in Castilla y León. Register the baby as soon as you have the birth registration.
  5. The application was made as a single-parent family, but the family does not qualify as one for this grant.
  6. A parent owes tax or Social Security payments, or the income could not be checked.
  7. The application is not signed. If you submit on paper, both parents must sign.
  8. It was submitted late.

If you disagree with the decision

The decision is final at the administrative stage. You have two options, counted from the day after it is notified or published:

  • An administrative appeal for reconsideration (recurso de reposición), within one month, to the same body that issued the decision. It is optional. It must be answered within a month; if there is no answer, it is deemed rejected.
  • A judicial appeal (recurso contencioso-administrativo) before the courts, within two months. If you lodged the reconsideration appeal first, you must wait until it is decided.

The reconsideration appeal is submitted like the application: via an electronic register or at a register office. General information on administrative appeals (PDF, Spanish).
