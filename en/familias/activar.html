Activate the card

The card won't work until you activate it. You only do this once, on the website of MoneyToPay (the company that issues it), and it takes about ten minutes.

Before you start

  • Use a computer if you can. MoneyToPay warns that some steps may not complete on a mobile phone.
  • It must be done by the person who applied for the grant (the applicant parent), using their own details.
  • Have the card, your DNI or NIE and your mobile phone to hand: you will receive a code by text message.
  • MoneyToPay's website is only in Spanish. Below, each button is quoted in Spanish with its meaning in brackets.

1. Create your MoneyToPay account

  1. Go to moneytopay.com and click «Regístrate» (Register)

    Open www.moneytopay.com. The button is at the top right.

  2. Fill in your personal details

    There are seven: DNI or NIE, first name, first and second surname, date of birth, nationality and country of residence.

  3. Enter your email address and mobile number

  4. Type in the code you receive by text message

    It is a one-time code that confirms the phone is yours.

  5. Create a password

    Keep it safe: you will need it to check your balance and transactions.

  6. Registration complete

    You now have an account. Next, link the card.

2. Link and activate the card

  1. Log in to your private area

    Sign in on moneytopay.com with your username and password.

  2. Choose «Vincular una tarjeta» (link a card) and then «Tarjeta Empresa»

    In the menu it may appear as «Activa tu tarjeta» (activate your card). «Tarjeta Empresa» is MoneyToPay's name for the Tarjeta Familia.

  3. Enter the card details and click «Activar» (activate)

    Copy them exactly as they appear on the card, type the characters shown in the image and accept the contract.

3. Check that it worked

  1. Log out and log back in

  2. Open «Mis tarjetas» (my cards)

    You should see a picture of the card and the available balance.

  3. To see transactions, click the three dots

    They are under the card number.

If you can see the card and its balance, it is active and you can start paying with it.

If it doesn't appear, it isn't linked to your ID and won't work yet. Repeat part 2 or call MoneyToPay.

Stuck?

Call MoneyToPay. It's free.

900 300 300

From abroad: +34 93 887 25 15

You can also write to them using their contact form (in Spanish).

MoneyToPay advertises 24-hour support every day, but also opening hours of 8:00 to 17:00, Monday to Friday.
