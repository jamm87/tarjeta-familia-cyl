Loss, theft and other problems

Find your situation below. For loss or theft, act quickly: there is a 24-hour deadline.

My card has been lost or stolen

Also if you think someone may have copied its number.

  1. Call CaixaBank on 900 40 40 90. It's free and open 24 hours. You must call within 24 hours of noticing.
  2. Email the Regional Ministry at conciliacion.gss@jcyl.es explaining what happened (in Spanish if you can).
  3. You will receive a new card at your home address.
I've been told a PIN is needed (in a shop or by CaixaBank)

That's not correct. The card has no PIN, although not all shop staff or helpline staff know this.

The Tarjeta Familia has no PIN

If the card terminal asks for a PIN, press the green key and the payment goes ahead. It is confirmed with your signature.

If staff in a shop, or even on the customer helpline, tell you that you need a PIN, you can show them the official terms of the Junta de Castilla y León:

«Se trata de una tarjeta de banda magnética, que no requiere PIN para realizar las compras (en caso de que se pida el PIN en el terminal de pago hay que pulsar la tecla verde para continuar la compra). Se autentica a través de firma. No es posible el uso mediante contacless.»

In English: “It is a magnetic stripe card that does not require a PIN for purchases (if the payment terminal asks for a PIN, press the green key to continue). It is authenticated by signature. Contactless use is not possible.”

Source: Terms and instructions for use of the Tarjeta Familia (Spanish), section 2 (16 October 2026).

If you still can't pay or the problem isn't resolved, email conciliacion.gss@jcyl.es saying where and when it happened.

The card doesn't work or is damaged

Call CaixaBank Customer Service as soon as possible on 900 40 40 90 (free) and email conciliacion.gss@jcyl.es. If it needs replacing, a new one will be sent to your home.

First, check that it is activated and has balance, and remember that it has no PIN and doesn't work contactless.

The shop declined the payment

The most common reasons are:

  • The amount is more than the balance. Check your balance and adjust the purchase.
  • They tried to take payment contactless. Ask them to swipe the stripe.
  • The terminal asked for a PIN and the green key wasn't pressed.
  • The shop's card terminal is not in an authorised business type.
  • The card is not yet activated, or has expired.
I can't register or link the card

Try from a computer: some steps may not complete on a mobile phone.

If you have already linked it but it doesn't appear under «Mis tarjetas» on the website, log in to the MoneyToPay app with the same account: sometimes it only shows there. You can also check the balance with the card details.

If it still doesn't work, call MoneyToPay on 900 300 300 (free) or write to them through their contact form.

The card has expired

The expiry date is printed on the card. After that date it stops working and any remaining balance can no longer be used. For any questions, call 012.

I returned a product: do I get the money back?

Not in cash. Purchases made with the card are not refunded in cash.
