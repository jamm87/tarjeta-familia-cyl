Frequently asked questions

What is the card's PIN?

There isn't one. If the terminal asks for it, press the green key and the payment goes ahead; it is confirmed with your signature.

This is stated in the official terms of use (section 2, in Spanish). If a shop or helpline tells you otherwise, see what to do.

Can I pay contactless or with my phone?

No. It only works by swiping the magnetic stripe.

Can I only shop at the listed shops?

No. You can also use it in any physical shop in Castilla y León in an authorised business type, even if it isn't a partner. The shop finder shows partner shops, which are those that have signed up voluntarily.

Can I shop online?

No, even if the shop accepts the card in store.

Can I use it outside Castilla y León?

No. Only in shops in Castilla y León.

Can I check my balance on my phone?

Yes, with the “MoneyToPay - Tarjeta prepago” app and your MoneyToPay account. Sometimes the card also shows in the app when it can't be seen on the website. How to do it.

Can I withdraw cash?

No. At CaixaBank cash machines you can only check the balance and transactions.

Who has to activate the card?

The person who applied for the grant (the applicant parent), registering with their own details. How to activate it.

Why does MoneyToPay call it “Tarjeta Regalo” or “Tarjeta Empresa”?

These are the generic names MoneyToPay, the issuer, uses for this type of prepaid card. It is the same Tarjeta Familia.

What happens if the purchase costs more than my balance?

The whole payment is declined. Check your balance first and keep the purchase within what you have left.

Do I need to keep receipts?

Yes, for 6 months from each purchase, with the products itemised. You may be asked for them to check how the money was spent. More information.

Does the balance expire?

Yes: it is no longer available after the expiry date printed on the card.

Who do I call if I have a problem?

It depends on the problem. Everything is in Loss, theft and other problems and in Help.
