Check your balance

It's worth checking before a large purchase: if the amount is more than your balance, the payment is declined.

On your phone, with the MoneyToPay app

The easiest way to check your balance before you pay.

  1. Download the “MoneyToPay - Tarjeta prepago” app: Google Play (Android) or App Store (iPhone).
  2. Log in with your MoneyToPay username and password, the same ones you created when you activated the card.
  3. Under “My Cards” you will see the card, the available balance and the latest transactions.

Can't see the card on the website? Sometimes the card doesn't appear on moneytopay.com but does appear when you log in to the app with the same account. Before assuming the card isn't linked, check the app or check the balance with the card details.

On the website, with your MoneyToPay account

  1. Log in on www.moneytopay.com with your username and password.
  2. Open «Mis tarjetas» (my cards): you will see the card and the available balance.
  3. The latest transactions are shown below. Click «Ver siguientes» (show more) to see earlier ones.

If the card doesn't appear under «Mis tarjetas»

The website may show «No tengo ninguna tarjeta MoneyToPay» (I don't have any MoneyToPay card) even though you have linked the card. It happened to us: the card was registered and working, but the website didn't show it.

You can still see the balance in two ways:

  • In the menu on the left, click «Consultar el saldo de tu tarjeta» (check your card balance), enter the number, expiry date and CVV2, tick «No soy un robot» and click «Consultar».
  • Log in to the MoneyToPay app with the same account: the card usually appears there.

If you still can't see the balance, call MoneyToPay on 900 300 300.

On the website, without an account

  1. On www.moneytopay.com, click «Consulta tu saldo» (check your balance).
  2. Enter the card details, tick «No soy un robot» (I'm not a robot) and click «Consultar» (check).

At a CaixaBank cash machine

You can check the balance and transactions at any CaixaBank cash machine. You can't withdraw money.
