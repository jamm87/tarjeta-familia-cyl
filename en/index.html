    The essentials

    • You must activate it on moneytopay.com before using it.
    • It works in physical shops in Castilla y León, for products and services for your children.
    • It has no PIN. If the terminal asks for one, press the green key. You pay by swiping the magnetic stripe and signing; contactless does not work.
    • Keep your receipts for 6 months.
    • If it is lost or stolen, call 900 40 40 90 within 24 hours.
